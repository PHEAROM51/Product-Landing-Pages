GO WHEN

The best night to visit in my opinion

Not all nights are the same at Tic Tic. Here's what I've noticed from going regularly.

MON

Cult Movie Mondays

Every Monday they screen a cult classic. The kind of film that didn't get enough attention when it came out but absolutely deserved it. Weird, bold, and always worth watching.

TUE

Asia Cine-Club

A night dedicated to Asian cinema. From Hong Kong classics to modern gems from Japan, Korea, and beyond. Great stories from perspectives you don't always get to see.

WED

Animation Wednesday

Animation done seriously. Studio Ghibli, classic Disney, independent shorts. Films that prove cartoons aren't just for kids. Relaxed, fun, emotional, and genuinely good every week.

Events

House Party

Tic Tic doesn't let a good occasion pass quietly. New Year's, holidays, and special dates get celebrated the right way, with themed nights, good music, and the kind of crowd that actually shows up to have a good time. Keep an eye on their page so you don't miss the next one.
